# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 29, 2026**

Conrado Oliveira (“I”) operates a personal, SMS-enabled assistant. This policy explains how information is handled when you communicate with the assistant by text.

## Information and use

When you request text messages, I may receive and use your phone number, the messages you send, and the messages sent in reply to provide the requested assistant service and respond to you. Do not send sensitive personal information by text.

## Messaging providers

Text messages are transmitted through telecommunications and messaging providers, including Twilio where applicable. Those providers process message and delivery information to carry the messages and operate their services, subject to their own policies. Wireless carriers may also process messages.

## Choices and opt-out

You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP**. You may also ask me during a conversation to stop messaging you. For help, reply **HELP** or contact me at [conrado@sunhavenpartnersllc.com](mailto:conrado@sunhavenpartnersllc.com).
